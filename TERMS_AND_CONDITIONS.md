## 2. What the System does — and does not do

The System is a questionnaire-based machine-learning screening and educational support tool for tuberculosis (TB) and HIV/AIDS. It analyses answers supplied by the user and produces an estimated result.

**The System is not a medical device, diagnostic service, laboratory test, or substitute for a qualified healthcare professional.** Its result is not a confirmed diagnosis and must not be used as the sole basis for treatment, medication, or other medical decisions. A low-risk result does not rule out infection, and a high-risk result does not prove infection.

If you have symptoms, believe you may have been exposed to TB or HIV, or are concerned about your health, seek advice and appropriate testing from a qualified healthcare provider or recognised health facility. Do not delay urgent medical care because of a result from this System.

Machine-learning outputs can be inaccurate, incomplete, or affected by the information supplied and the limitations of the underlying data and models. The System may be updated, changed, interrupted, or unavailable.

## 5. Information we collect and save

Depending on the features you use, the System may collect or process:

### Account information
- Full name, where supplied.
- Email address and account identifiers.
- Authentication and account-management information. Password authentication is handled through the connected authentication service; the prediction-history record stores the user's email rather than their password.

### Questionnaire and health-related information
- Answers to TB symptom questions, which may include fever, cough, coughing blood, night sweats, chest or back pain, breathlessness, weight loss, tiredness, lumps or swelling, swollen lymph nodes, and appetite changes.
- HIV/AIDS questionnaire information, which may include age, marital status, education, HIV/AIDS awareness, sexual orientation, places where sexual partners are met, drug-taking information, history of sexually transmitted diseases, and whether an HIV test was taken in the past year.

Some of these answers may reveal health information, sexual-life information, or other sensitive personal information. Treat them as confidential. Only submit information you are comfortable providing and that is needed to use the relevant screening feature.

### Prediction and history information
When a prediction is run, the System saves the account email, selected disease, submitted questionnaire input, prediction result, and associated probability in order to provide the Prediction History feature and support authorised system administration.

### Feedback and technical information
If you submit feedback, the System saves the feedback together with the account email. The hosting, authentication, and database services may also process technical information needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the service, subject to their own terms and privacy notices.
