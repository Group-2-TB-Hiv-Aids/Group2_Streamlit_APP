## 2. Purpose and limitations of the System

The System is a questionnaire-based machine-learning screening and educational support platform for tuberculosis (TB) and HIV/AIDS. It analyses information entered by users and provides an estimated screening result.

The System does not provide a confirmed medical diagnosis, laboratory testing, treatment, or professional medical advice. Its results must not be used as the sole basis for medical decisions, treatment, or medication. A low-risk result does not rule out infection, and a high-risk result does not establish that a person has an infection.

If you have symptoms, believe you may have been exposed to TB or HIV, or have concerns about your health, consult a qualified healthcare professional or recognised health facility and obtain appropriate testing. Do not delay medical care because of a result produced by the System.

Machine-learning results may be inaccurate or incomplete. Their reliability depends on the information supplied and the limitations of the datasets and models. The System may be updated, interrupted, or temporarily unavailable.

## 4. Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, the System collects and processes the following information.

### Account information
- Your email address and account identifier.
- Your name, where supplied.
- Information required to authenticate and manage your account. Your password is handled by the connected authentication service and is not stored as plain text in the prediction-history records.

### Questionnaire and health-related information
For TB screening, this may include answers about fever, cough, coughing blood, night sweats, chest or back pain, breathlessness, weight loss, tiredness, lumps or swelling, swollen lymph nodes, and appetite changes.

For HIV/AIDS screening, this may include age, marital status, education, HIV/AIDS awareness, sexual orientation, places where sexual partners are met, drug-taking information, history of sexually transmitted diseases, and whether an HIV test was taken in the past year.

Some questionnaire answers may reveal health information, sexual-life information, or other sensitive personal information. Please consider this before submitting your answers.

### Prediction and history information
When you submit a prediction, the System records information associated with your account, including your email address, selected disease, questionnaire responses, prediction result, and associated probability. These records support the Prediction History feature, allow you to review previous results, and support authorised system administration.

### Feedback and technical information
If you submit feedback, the System stores the feedback with your account email address. The hosting, authentication, and database services may process technical information required to operate, protect, troubleshoot, and maintain the System.
